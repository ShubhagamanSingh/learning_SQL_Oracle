# Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026

**Last updated:** 28 October 2026

This notice describes privacy practices directly controlled by **Shubhagaman Singh** for the **Oracle SQL and PL/SQL Learning Resources** project. It does not replace the privacy notices of GitHub, email providers, website hosts, or other third-party services.

## Information the project may receive

The repository itself does not run a custom account system, analytics service, database, cookie banner, or newsletter.

The maintainer may receive information that you voluntarily provide, including:

- your GitHub username, public profile, issues, discussions, pull requests, commits, and review comments;
- your name and email address when included in Git metadata or messages;
- correspondence sent to the project email address; and
- technical or contextual information you include in a report.

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, private keys, confidential records, or unnecessary personal data.

## How information may be used

Information may be used to:

- respond to questions and reports;
- review, attribute, and maintain contributions;
- investigate abuse, copyright, privacy, or security concerns;
- protect the Project and its community; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## Public information

GitHub issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, and profiles may be public and copied by others. Removing content from the current repository may not remove forks, clones, caches, archives, or Git history.

## Sharing

The maintainer does not sell personal information received through this Project. Information may be handled by hosting and communication providers, disclosed when required by law, shared to protect rights or safety, or published when you submit it to a public channel.

## Retention and requests

Project correspondence and contribution records may be retained as reasonably needed for maintenance, attribution, dispute resolution, safety, and legal compliance. Requests to correct or remove information controlled by the maintainer may be sent to [shubhagamansingh@gmail.com](mailto:shubhagamansingh@gmail.com). Some records may need to remain for legal, integrity, attribution, or technical reasons.

## Sample data in lessons

Names, phone numbers, email addresses, account identifiers, and similar values appearing in examples are presented as historical classroom or illustrative data. They must not be treated as verified contact details or used for outreach. If any example identifies you or contains data that should not be public, contact the maintainer with the exact file and line.

## Children

The Project is an educational repository intended for a general learning audience and does not knowingly operate a service designed to collect children’s personal information. Learners should avoid publishing personal information and should use appropriate adult or institutional guidance where required.

## Third-party services

Review the applicable third-party policies before using GitHub, following external links, sending email, or visiting <https://shubhagaman.in>. This notice covers only Project activity directly controlled by the maintainer; the website may publish a separate notice for website-specific processing.
