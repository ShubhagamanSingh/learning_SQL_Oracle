# Security Policy

This repository primarily contains educational documentation and SQL/PLSQL examples. Security issues may still include exposed credentials or personal data, unsafe instructions, malicious links, or vulnerabilities in future scripts or automation.

## Report privately

Do **not** open a public issue for a sensitive vulnerability.

Email **Shubhagaman Singh** at [shubhagamansingh@gmail.com](mailto:shubhagamansingh@gmail.com) with:

- a clear description of the issue;
- the affected file, URL, commit, or line;
- reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- likely impact;
- any suggested remediation; and
- whether disclosure timing is sensitive.

Do not include real credentials, unnecessary personal data, destructive payloads, or data obtained through unauthorized access.

## Safe-harbor expectations

Act in good faith, respect privacy, avoid service disruption or data destruction, use only accounts and systems you are authorized to test, and allow reasonable time for review before public disclosure. This policy does not authorize testing of GitHub, Oracle, <https://shubhagaman.in>, or any third-party system.

The maintainer will try to acknowledge a report and communicate next steps, but no guaranteed response or remediation timeline is offered.

## Supported content

The latest commit on the default branch is the maintained version. Historical `.txt` files are archival material; security corrections may be documented in their Markdown companions rather than rewriting the historical transcript.

General technical corrections that do not expose a vulnerability may be submitted through a normal issue or pull request.
