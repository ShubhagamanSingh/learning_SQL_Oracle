# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful learning environment regardless of experience level, education, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, or other protected or personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain corrections without ridicule.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property rights.
- Avoid publishing credentials, personal data, or harmful instructions.
- Accept reasonable moderation and correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized conduct;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption;
- spam, impersonation, deceptive attribution, or false claims of endorsement;
- publishing another person’s private information without permission; or
- knowingly introducing malicious code, unsafe instructions, or secrets.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **Shubhagaman Singh** at [shubhagamansingh@gmail.com](mailto:shubhagamansingh@gmail.com). Include relevant links or screenshots and avoid sharing more personal information than necessary.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Confidentiality will be respected as far as reasonably possible, but complete confidentiality or a particular outcome cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, limit participation, or refer conduct to the hosting platform or appropriate authority, depending on context and severity. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is unacceptable.

This code governs Project-managed community spaces. Third-party platforms also enforce their own policies.
